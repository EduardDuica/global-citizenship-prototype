About Website name

Not every student has the same chance of getting a good education. Many cannot afford private tutoring, rural schools may have few resources, and teachers have limited time for each learner. We want every student to succeed, wherever they live.

Who we help

Our main users are students from low-income or rural families. Parents and teachers who support them are our secondary users.

Why we are different

Most online resources need a subscription. Website name is free, and the full site is designed to work offline so students with unreliable internet can still learn.
